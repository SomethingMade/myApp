What we serve

    Fire first, then the glass.

    Shaka started as a grill with a good bar attached. Now it is a bar with a serious grill. Everything meaty goes over open coals, and everything liquid is poured by someone who can tell you where it came from.

    Come for a long lunch, a birthday table of twelve, or one drink at the counter. Nobody will rush you.
